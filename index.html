05 / WHY FINFAIR?

Technology should make important decisions easier.

FinFair is an early-stage student project exploring how simple, transparent calculations can help prospective university students understand financial tradeoffs. It is a prototype—not a replacement for official university cost estimates or professional financial advice.

All figures are user-entered or illustrative. No account, backend, tracking, or financial data collection is used.

BEHIND THE PROJECT

Why I Built FinFair

Planning for university involves more than choosing where to study. Students also need to understand tuition, living expenses, and how different financial decisions can affect their future.

I created FinFair to make financial planning clearer and more accessible for students. By combining budgeting tools, university cost comparisons, and currency conversion, I wanted to help students explore their options with greater confidence.

UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

Student Research in Progress

To better understand the financial challenges students face when planning for university, I created a student survey to explore their budgeting habits, financial concerns, and awareness of university expenses.

This research is an ongoing part of FinFair. My goal is to use student feedback to identify useful features, improve the website, and make financial planning more accessible.

FROM IDEA TO EXECUTION

My Development Journey

01. Turning an Idea into a Project

FinFair began with a simple question: How can technology make university financial planning easier for students? I started by identifying useful tools and planning how they could work together on one platform.

02. Building the Platform

Using HTML and JavaScript, I developed budgeting tools, university cost comparisons, USD/BDT currency conversion, and projected financial balances. I also worked through coding errors and connected different components to create a functioning website.

03. Testing and Improving

I ran 21 automated tests to check FinFair's functionality. Debugging and testing helped me understand the importance of accuracy, problem-solving, and continuous improvement in software development.

04. Continuing the Project

FinFair is an ongoing project. I plan to continue improving its features, gathering student feedback, and exploring ways to make financial information easier to understand.

MEASURING PROGRESS

Impact & Future Goals

Making Financial Planning Accessible

My goal with FinFair is to help students understand the financial side of university planning through simple, interactive tools. Rather than relying on complicated calculations, students can explore possible expenses and compare different financial scenarios.

Measuring Real Impact

I am working to gather student feedback and understand which features are most useful. As the project develops, I hope to measure its impact through survey responses, user experiences, and improvements made based on student suggestions.

What's Next for FinFair?

My next goals include improving the university comparison experience, making the financial tools more intuitive, and using research findings to guide future development.

FinFair is more than a completed coding project. It is an opportunity for me to continue learning, solving problems, and building technology that can help others.
